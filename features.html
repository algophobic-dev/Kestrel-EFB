Everything in Kestrel EFB

Charts, weather, flight plans, plates and records. All of it is free.

Maps

  • FAA VFR sectional, VFR terminal, IFR low and IFR high charts
  • open flightmaps VFR charts for about 20 countries in Europe and southern Africa
  • Airspace, obstacles and terrain hazard coloring
  • Animated radar, flight categories, SIGMETs and advisories
  • Radar, SIGMETs and world airspace outside the US
  • Live traffic
  • Runway and taxiway charts when you zoom in
  • Dark, light, topographic and satellite base maps

Flight plan

  • Routes with airways, SIDs, STARs and approaches
  • Suggested airway routes between two airports
  • NavLog with winds aloft, climb and descent
  • Terrain, obstacle and airspace profile
  • 3D view along the route
  • Print the NavLog

Airports

  • About 57,000 airports worldwide
  • METAR, TAF and D-ATIS
  • Runways with headwind and crosswind
  • Frequencies, elevation and position
  • Your own fuel price notes
  • One button opens FAA NOTAM Search for the airport

Plates

  • Current FAA approach plates, departures, arrivals and airport diagrams
  • Approach plates placed on the map at their true position
  • Save an airport's plates for offline use

Terrain in 3D

Play the route from takeoff to landing, or follow your GPS position. Terrain within 1,000 ft of your altitude turns yellow, and within 100 ft it turns red.

Also in the app

Flights

Weight and balance, fuel planning, and takeoff and landing distance estimates for each flight.

Logbook

Log each flight and see your totals.

Checklists

Write your own checklists. The app can read each item aloud.

ScratchPads

Draw or type a clearance, ATIS or taxi route.

Offline charts

Download charts for an area and use them with no connection.

Sync

Sign in with a username and password to keep your data on every device. No email needed.

Documents

Import PDFs and keep them in the app.

Track logs

Record your flight and export it as GPX.

Menu bar

The Mac and Windows apps add menus and keyboard shortcuts for every tab and map layer.
